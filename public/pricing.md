## 1. Outpatient (OPD) Consultation Fees
- **New Patient Orthopaedic Consultation**: Standard nominal OPD fee (payable at clinic desk; typically ₹400 – ₹600).
- **Online Booking / Pre-registration Fee**: ₹0 (Zero advance payment required). Patients can request an appointment online or via WhatsApp without entering financial or card details.
- **Follow-up Consultation**: Validated follow-up window (typically within 7–10 days) provided at discounted or follow-up rates for review of diagnostic imaging (X-rays, MRI, blood work).

## 2. In-Clinic Minor Procedures & Immobilization
- **Plaster / Splint Application (Fracture Management)**: Nominal charges based on material (Plaster of Paris / POP vs. Lightweight Fiberglass) and limb extent (slab vs. full cylindrical cast).
- **Joint / Soft Tissue Injection (when clinically indicated)**: Medication/steroid/viscosupplement cost plus standard procedure administration fee.
- **Wound Dressing & Suture Removal**: Standard in-clinic nursing & minor surgical care rate.

## 3. Inpatient Surgical Care & Hospital Admissions
- **Surgical Procedures**: Including Arthroscopy (Knee / Shoulder), Open Reduction & Internal Fixation (ORIF) for fractures, and Joint Reconstruction.
- **Facility**: Elective and emergency surgeries are performed at accredited partner hospitals in Agra (including Agra Ortho & Trauma Care Center and Apex Multispeciality Hospital).
- **Pricing**: Transparent surgical package estimation provided prior to admission based on room category, implant type, and hospital stay duration.

## 4. Health Insurance & Cashless TPA Policy
- **Cashless Mediclaim**: Accepted for hospital admissions and surgical interventions via accredited partner hospital TPA desks.
- **Major Insurers Supported**: Star Health, Care Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Niva Bupa, Bajaj Allianz, New India Assurance, Oriental Insurance, United India Insurance, and corporate TPAs (Medi Assist, Paramount, MDIndia, Heritage, FHPL).
- **Documentation Support**: Complete assistance provided for pre-authorization queries, discharge summaries, and medical reimbursement claim dossiers.

## 5. Accepted Payment Modes (At Clinic)
- **UPI**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM
- **Cards**: All major Credit Cards and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
- **Cash**: Accepted at clinic reception desk
- **Online Advance Requirement**: None (pay-on-arrival model)

## 6. Diagnostic Investigations Policy
- **X-Rays & Imaging**: Clinical evaluation is conducted first. If X-rays, MRI, or CT scans are required, patients are provided with a written prescription and are free to utilize any NABH/NABL-accredited diagnostic center of their choice. No mandatory lab lock-in.

## 7. Emergency & Acute Trauma Notice
- For polytrauma, unstable cervical spine injuries, severe head trauma, or bleeding open compound fractures requiring immediate Intensive Care (ICU) and round-the-clock emergency surgical theatres, patients must report immediately to the nearest 24/7 tertiary trauma hospital.
